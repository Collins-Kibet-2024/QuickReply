# Company Holidays

## Paid holidays

Lumen Systems observes 11 paid holidays each year:

- New Year's Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Presidents' Day
- Memorial Day
- Juneteenth
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day and the Friday after
- Christmas Eve and Christmas Day

## Floating holidays

In addition to the fixed holidays, every employee receives 2 floating holidays per calendar year. Floating holidays can be used for any personal, cultural, or religious observance. They must be used by December 31 and do not carry over or pay out.

## Holidays that fall on a weekend

When a holiday falls on a Saturday, it is observed on the preceding Friday. When it falls on a Sunday, it is observed on the following Monday.

## Working on a holiday

Employees in on-call or support roles who are scheduled to work on a company holiday receive an alternate day off, to be taken within 60 days. Non-exempt employees are also paid 1.5 times their regular rate for hours worked on the holiday.
