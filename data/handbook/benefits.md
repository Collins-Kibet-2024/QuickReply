# Benefits

## Health insurance

Lumen Systems offers three medical plans: a PPO, an HMO, and a high-deductible health plan (HDHP) paired with a health savings account (HSA). Lumen pays 85% of employee premiums and 70% of dependent premiums. Dental and vision coverage are included at no cost to employees.

## Open enrollment

Benefits open enrollment runs each year from November 1 through November 15. Elections take effect on January 1. Outside open enrollment, you can only change your elections within 30 days of a qualifying life event, such as marriage, birth or adoption of a child, or loss of other coverage.

## 401(k) retirement plan

Employees are eligible to join the 401(k) plan on their first day. Lumen matches 100% of the first 4% of salary you contribute, plus 50% of the next 2%. Employer matching contributions vest over three years: 33% after year one, 66% after year two, and 100% after year three.

## Wellness and other benefits

- **Wellness stipend:** $600 per year for gym memberships, fitness classes, or mental health apps.
- **Employee Assistance Program (EAP):** free, confidential counseling sessions, available 24/7.
- **Life and disability insurance:** basic life insurance at 1x annual salary and short- and long-term disability coverage, paid in full by Lumen.
- **Learning budget:** $1,500 per year for courses, conferences, and certifications.
