# Paid Time Off (PTO)

## Eligibility

All regular full-time employees of Lumen Systems are eligible for paid time off (PTO) from their first day of employment. Part-time employees working at least 20 hours per week accrue PTO on a prorated basis. Contractors and interns are not eligible.

## Accrual

PTO accrues each pay period based on years of service:

| Years of service | Annual PTO |
|---|---|
| 0–2 years | 15 days |
| 3–5 years | 20 days |
| 6+ years | 25 days |

Employees may carry over up to 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year. Any balance above the carryover cap is forfeited on January 1.

## Requesting time off

Submit PTO requests through the Lumen self-service portal at least two weeks in advance for absences of three or more days. Shorter absences should be requested as early as possible. Your manager approves or declines requests within three business days.

## Sick time

Sick time is tracked separately from PTO. Employees receive 8 sick days per year, which do not carry over. A doctor's note is only required for absences longer than three consecutive workdays.

## Payout at separation

Accrued, unused PTO is paid out in the final paycheck when an employee leaves Lumen, unless state law requires otherwise. Sick time is not paid out.
