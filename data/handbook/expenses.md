# Expenses and Reimbursement

## What is reimbursable

Lumen Systems reimburses reasonable, necessary business expenses, including travel, client meals, conference fees, and approved software or equipment. Personal expenses, alcohol beyond a client meal, traffic tickets, and first-class airfare are not reimbursable.

## How to submit an expense report

1. Log in to the Lumen expense portal.
2. Create a new expense report and add one line per expense.
3. Attach an itemized receipt for every expense of $25 or more.
4. Submit the report to your manager for approval.

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Approved reimbursements are paid by direct deposit within 10 business days.

## Travel

Book flights and hotels through the company travel portal. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. The daily meal allowance while traveling is $75 in the US. Personal car mileage is reimbursed at the current federal standard rate.

## Remote work equipment

Remote employees receive a one-time $500 home office stipend for furniture and accessories, plus a $50 monthly internet stipend. Laptops and monitors are provided by IT and remain company property.
