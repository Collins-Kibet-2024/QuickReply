# Parental Leave

## Eligibility

All regular employees who have been with Lumen Systems for at least 90 days are eligible for paid parental leave. The policy applies equally to all parents, including birth parents, non-birth parents, adoptive parents, and foster parents.

## Paid leave

- **Birth parents** receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave.
- **Non-birth, adoptive, and foster parents** receive 12 weeks of fully paid leave.

Leave must be taken within 12 months of the child's birth or placement. It may be taken all at once or in up to two blocks of at least 2 weeks each.

## Relationship to FMLA

Eligible US employees may also be covered by the Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA), which provides job-protected leave. Lumen's paid parental leave runs concurrently with FMLA leave where both apply. Employees who are not yet FMLA-eligible still receive Lumen's paid parental leave.

## Returning to work

Employees returning from parental leave may phase back in at 60% of their regular schedule, with full pay, for up to 4 weeks. Lactation rooms are available in every Lumen office.

## How to request leave

Notify your manager and HR at least 30 days before your expected leave start date when possible. Submit the leave request form in the Lumen self-service portal. Questions can be sent to hr@lumen.example.
